# NLQM Notes

## Project Start

This side quest begins after the IARD 2026 talk. The talk led naturally to a
deeper question: what is the historical and conceptual development of nonlinear
quantum mechanics, and how should Weinberg 1989 be understood in that history?

The project will proceed historically and step by step.

Initial correction: the intended focal paper is Weinberg **1989**, not 1985.

## Working Orientation

Weinberg 1989 should be treated neither as an endorsement of nonlinear quantum
mechanics nor as a dismissal of it. It is a disciplined framework for making
nonlinear corrections precise enough to analyze and test.

Working formulation:

> Weinberg's 1989 paper was less a proposal that quantum mechanics is nonlinear
> than a disciplined way to make "nonlinear quantum mechanics" precise enough to
> test.

## Initial Questions

```text
What earlier nonlinear quantum theories existed before Weinberg?
What problem was Weinberg trying to solve?
Why did he insist on homogeneity/ray-dependence?
How did he define observables and eigenvalues?
How did he treat separated systems?
What exactly did Polchinski show was wrong or dangerous?
How does the later QFT/Tomonaga-Schwinger discussion relate back to Weinberg?
```
