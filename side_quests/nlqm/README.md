# NLQM Side Quest

NLQM means nonlinear quantum mechanics.

This side quest is for the Weinberg 1989 rabbit hole and the broader topic of
nonlinearities in quantum theory, including:

```text
Weinberg 1989
Gisin and Polchinski
Ho-Hsu 2014
Hsu 2026
Diósi's 2026 comment
Kaplan-Rajendran retarded nonlinear QM
Tomonaga-Schwinger covariance and integrability
state-dependent Hamiltonian nonlinearities
separability, no-signaling, and nonlinear dynamics
```
